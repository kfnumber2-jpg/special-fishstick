Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how APEX Performance ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our site and purchase a training package. This is a starting template — review it with a qualified professional before relying on it.

Information we collect

  • Account & contact info — your name and email when you sign up or purchase.
  • Payment info — processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your full card details on our servers.
  • Usage data — basic analytics about how you use the site, to improve the experience.

How we use it

  • To deliver the training course and your chosen package.
  • To process payments and send receipts and account information.
  • To respond to support requests and send relevant updates.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate — such as Stripe (payments) and our hosting/email providers — and only as needed. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you can opt out of marketing emails at any time.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email coach@apexperformance.com.
